## Attack Description
If an attacker conducts a successful brute force attack, they are rewarded with a valid set of credentials which can subsequently be used as a means of initial access. This detection scenario is a continuation of [SSH Brute Force](https://github.com/benimbriaco1/Wazuh-Homelab/blob/main/Detections/SSH%20Brute%20Force/Detection.md)

## Attack Scenario
To simulate this attack, I created a wordlist with 1000 words in it, one of them being the correct password.
